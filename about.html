I am studying Aerospace Engineering at the University of Texas at Austin. I love examining the workings of cars, planes, rockets, all things that move fast essentially, which is why I like projects that bring mechanical design, iteration, and fabrication together.

I put together these projects because, first and foremost, it's fun, that's the most important part. However, it also teaches me how the things I see and use every day are actually designed and put together.

This website documents the projects that I'm proud of and explains what design decisions went into them, and what I've learned.
